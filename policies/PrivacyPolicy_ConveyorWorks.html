Privacy Policy — Conveyor Works

Last updated: October 10, 2026 · Android package: com.easycube.conveyorworks

Easy Cube Games develops Conveyor Works, a factory-building game about extracting ore, connecting machines and completing production contracts. This policy explains the information the game uses and how to contact us. Contact: easycubeindiegames@gmail.com.

Your choice before gameplay

On first launch, the game asks you to accept this policy and allow gameplay analytics and crash reporting. The window has a single Accept button; acceptance is required to enter gameplay. We save this decision on your device before starting these services. If saving fails, the window remains open and you can try again. There are no separate in-game analytics or crash-reporting switches. You can read this policy from that window and from Settings.

Events before acceptance are not queued for later delivery. In preview builds without a Firebase project configuration, analytics and crash-reporting services remain inactive even after acceptance.

Game progress and local storage

No account, sign-in, player name or cloud save is required. Your six campaign factory layouts and workshop, machine contents, separate in-game budgets, task and order progress, active deliveries, medals and records, active play time, unlocked blueprints, language, sound and effects preferences, tutorial state and camera positions are stored locally. The privacy decision is stored separately on your device.

The Android app also keeps a rotating local campaign event journal for playtesting, containing the campaign event names and aggregate parameters described below. It is stored on the device and is not automatically uploaded. A previous journal is retained when the current file exceeds 2 MiB; clearing app storage removes these files.

Resetting progress removes the local factory and gameplay progress, but does not revoke privacy acceptance or delete data already processed by Firebase. The game can be played offline; it does not calculate production while it is closed. Android backup and restore services, if enabled in your device settings, may handle application data separately. Local progress is not a server account.

Analytics and crash reports

After acceptance, configured Android builds use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics:

  • Gameplay analytics: automatic app and session events, the beginning of the tutorial, completed tasks, task rewards, accepted contracts and contract rewards, campaign and chapter starts and completions, order starts, completions and cancellations, and hint usage. Custom event parameters contain task, chapter or order identifiers, the selected language, active play durations, simulation tick counts, attempt numbers, balance version, legacy-profile migration status, medals and hint usage counts/levels. They do not contain the raw save, factory layout, names, email addresses or payment details.
  • Device and installation information: app version, device and operating-system information, language, app-instance or installation identifiers and technical service information. Analytics can derive an approximate region from IP addresses. The game does not request precise location access.
  • Crash reports: crash traces, exceptions, device and application state and technical diagnostic information. Analytics may supply activity breadcrumbs that help explain a crash.

These data help us understand progression, improve balance and diagnose errors. Advertising ID collection and advertising personalization signals are disabled. The game does not set a custom user ID.

See Firebase privacy information, Firebase Android data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy for provider information.

Advertising, purchases and social features

This version has no advertising SDK, real-money purchases, payment collection, player chat, live multiplayer or user uploads. Money shown with a dollar symbol is an in-game resource and cannot be redeemed for real money. New services that change these uses require updated app disclosures and an updated policy.

Service providers and security

Google processes analytics and diagnostic data to provide Firebase services. We do not sell your personal information. Providers may process data outside your country under their applicable terms and safeguards. Firebase service communications use encrypted connections. No storage or transmission method guarantees complete security.

Retention and managing data

Local progress remains until reset or removed, subject to Android storage and backup behavior. Firebase applies its own retention rules. Analytics retention depends on the project's configuration and report type; aggregated reports can follow different rules. Crashlytics describes a 90-day period before it begins removing applicable crash reports and installation identifiers from live and backup systems. This does not mean all game-related data are erased within 90 days.

Uninstalling the game, clearing app storage or resetting game progress does not automatically erase information already processed by Firebase. When you stop running the app, it sends no new game events from that installation. Previously processed information remains subject to provider retention rules.

For questions or a request concerning your data, contact easycubeindiegames@gmail.com and identify Conveyor Works. There is no account, and an email address alone may not identify records associated with a Firebase installation identifier. We will explain what information is needed and what actions are possible under applicable law and provider tools. The game has no automatic server-data deletion feature. Do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary sensitive information.

Support, children and external links

If you contact support, we use the information you provide to answer and investigate your request. The game does not ask for a date of birth. A parent or guardian with a concern about a child's information can contact us at the address above. Store age ratings describe game content separately from privacy and consent requirements.

Links open external pages with their own privacy practices. Opening a policy link does not change your saved privacy decision.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. The date above identifies the version. Materially different data purposes require an updated in-game privacy decision. Questions: easycubeindiegames@gmail.com.
