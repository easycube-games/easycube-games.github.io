Information Collection and Use

Spellarium: Magic Clicker does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide any personally identifiable information directly (such as your name, email address, or phone number). Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

The app uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information used to identify your device and understand how the app is used. This includes:

  • Google Play Services

Game analytics starts after you press Accept in the initial Privacy screen. Acceptance is required to access gameplay.
  • CAS.AI and its advertising partners — serve advertisements and measure ad interactions. Depending on your advertising choices, these services may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address and advertising activity.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — collects crash reports and related technical information (device state at the time of a crash, device model, operating system version, crash traces) so we can find and fix stability problems.

Advertising and Purchases

The app offers optional rewarded advertisements, interstitial ad breaks, and one-time purchases through Google Play using Unity IAP. Advertising preferences are managed by CAS Consent Flow and can be revisited in Settings → Advertising preferences. These preferences are separate from the initial game analytics acceptance.

Google Play processes payments. We do not collect your payment card details. Purchase entitlements and a local transaction journal are stored on your device to restore benefits and prevent repeated resource grants on that installation. There is no game account or cloud save: restoring a permanent purchase does not restore your local resource balances.

When game analytics is enabled, purchase outcomes and advertising revenue measurements are recorded in Firebase. Our analytics events do not include purchase receipts, purchase tokens, email addresses or payment details. A local queue of permitted monetization events contains at most 1,000 events for up to seven days. No game analytics events are queued before acceptance. Data already processed by service providers is subject to their policies linked above.
