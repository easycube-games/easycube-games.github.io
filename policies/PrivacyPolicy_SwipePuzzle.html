Privacy Policy — Swipe Puzzle

Last updated: October 4, 2026 · Android package: com.easycube.swipepuzzle

Easy Cube Games develops Swipe Puzzle, a single-player puzzle game in which you collect shapes with swipes. This policy explains the information used by the game and by the services it relies on, why it is used, and how to contact us. Contact: easycubeindiegames@gmail.com.

Game progress and local storage

No account or sign-in is required, and the game does not ask for your name, email address or other contact details. Your current level, the number of levels you have started, whether the game has been launched before and the record of a "No ads" purchase are stored on your device. Android device backups, if enabled by your device or system settings, may handle application data separately from our game.

Advertising

The game is free and supported by ads from Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): a banner at the top of the screen and occasional full-screen ads when a level starts. To show, limit, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, Unity Ads may process the advertising identifier and other device identifiers, the IP address and an approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. Unity's privacy policy applies to this processing and describes your choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings.

Purchases

The game offers one optional one-time purchase, "No ads", which turns off the banner and full-screen ads. It is made through Google Play using Unity In-App Purchasing. Google Play processes the payment; we do not receive your card or payment details. The purchase is recorded on your device, and Google Play keeps it in your Google account.

Unity services and diagnostics

The game is built with Unity. Besides advertising and purchases, Unity's engine services enabled in the game can send device information (such as model, operating system and language), the app version, installation identifiers, basic usage events such as app starts, and crash reports to Unity. This helps keep the game working and shows, in aggregate, how it is used.

Service providers and security

Google (Google Play and Google Play services) and Unity Technologies process information to provide their services under their own terms. We do not sell your personal information. Service providers may process data on servers outside your country under their applicable terms and safeguards. Network communications use encrypted connections. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

Provider information: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, Unity Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and managing data

Local data remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Unity and Google keep the data they process under their own retention rules. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data stops new collection from that installation but does not automatically erase information already processed by service providers.

Contact easycubeindiegames@gmail.com with privacy questions or a request concerning your data, and name Swipe Puzzle in the message. Because there is no account and the services use device and installation identifiers, an email address alone may not identify an installation's records; we will explain what information is needed and what action is possible under the applicable law and the providers' tools. Please do not send passwords or payment information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not ask for a date of birth. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the address above.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its services change, for example when a new service is added. The date above identifies the current version. Questions: easycubeindiegames@gmail.com.
