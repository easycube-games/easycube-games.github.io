Privacy Policy — Swipe Sprint

Last updated: October 4, 2026 · Android package: com.easycube.swipepuzzle · Up to version 0.8.0 the game was called Swipe Puzzle

Easy Cube Games develops Swipe Sprint, a single-player reaction game in which you find a shape on a board of colour tiles and swipe over it before the time runs out. This policy explains the information used by the game, why it is used, and how to contact us. Contact: easycubeindiegames@gmail.com.

The policy describes version 1.0.0 and later. The section "Earlier versions" describes the versions published as Swipe Puzzle.

Your choice before playing

On the first launch, the game shows a privacy notice with one Accept button. Pressing Accept accepts this policy and allows game analytics, crash reports and advertising, including personalized ads. Acceptance is required to play. We save this decision on your device before any of these services start. There are no separate in-game switches for analytics, crash reports or advertising. You can read this policy from the first-launch notice and at any time from Settings → Privacy Policy; opening the policy does not change your decision.

Before acceptance, the game does not start the analytics or crash-reporting services and does not collect gameplay events to send them later.

Game progress and local storage

No account, sign-in or cloud save is required, and the game does not ask for your name, email address or other contact details. The levels you have reached and the stars earned on them, your best streak and Blitz record, your settings (sound, vibration, effects, tile symbols and colours, language), the counters the game uses to space out ad breaks, your privacy decision and the record of a "No ads" purchase are stored on your device. Android device backups, if enabled by your device or system settings, may handle application data separately from our game.

Analytics and crash reports

After acceptance, the game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics:

  • Analytics: app sessions; the start, continuation and result of a level (level number, chapter, board size, stars, score and target, mistakes, best streak, number of shapes, time left); the start, continuation and result of a Blitz round; when an ad or its placeholder is shown and how it ends; and the start and result of a purchase (product and outcome). Our events do not include your name, email address, purchase receipts, purchase tokens or payment details.
  • Device and installation information: app version, device model, operating system, language, app-instance and installation identifiers, the advertising identifier where available, and technical service information. Analytics can derive an approximate region from IP addresses. The game does not request location permissions.
  • Crash reports: crash traces, errors, application and device state at the time of the problem, and the number of the level being played.

These data help us understand how the game is played, balance the levels and fix errors. Firebase Analytics may also record purchases made through Google Play automatically.

Advertising

The current version shows no ads. Where an ad will appear in a later update — between rounds and when you choose to continue a lost round — the game shows a short placeholder screen instead. The advertising component built into the app (the CAS.AI SDK) is not started in this version, and the game passes no data to it.

A later update will turn advertising on: a short ad break between rounds and an optional rewarded video to continue a lost round, served through the CAS.AI mediation platform and the advertising networks it works with. Such services may process the advertising identifier and other device identifiers, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to deliver, limit, measure and personalize ads. We will update this policy before that update is released. The acceptance described above already covers advertising, so the game will not ask again.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings.

Purchases

The game has one optional one-time purchase, "No ads", made through Google Play using Unity In-App Purchasing. While the game shows no ads, the purchase is not offered for sale. A purchase made in an earlier version stays valid: the game checks it with Google Play and keeps it on your device, together with a hash of its transaction number so that it is not granted twice. Google Play restores the purchase on the same Google account after reinstalling. Google Play processes payments; we do not receive your card or payment details.

Service providers and security

Google (Firebase, Google Play) and Unity Technologies (In-App Purchasing) process information to provide their services. The game is built with the Unity engine; Unity's own analytics, advertising and crash-reporting services are switched off in this version. We do not sell your personal information. Service providers may process data on servers outside your country under their applicable terms and safeguards. Network communications use encrypted connections. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

Provider information: Firebase privacy information, Firebase Android data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy, Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, CAS.AI Privacy Policy.

Retention and managing data

Local data remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, subject to Android storage and backup behavior. Firebase data follow their own retention rules: Analytics retention depends on the project's retention settings and the type of report, and Crashlytics describes a 90-day period before it begins removing applicable crash reports and installation identifiers. This is not a promise that all game-related data are deleted within 90 days.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data stops new collection from that installation but does not automatically erase information already processed by service providers. Contact easycubeindiegames@gmail.com with privacy questions or a request concerning your data, and name Swipe Sprint in the message. Because there is no account and the services use installation identifiers, an email address alone may not identify an installation's records; we will explain what information is needed and what action is possible under the applicable law and the providers' tools. Please do not send passwords or payment information.

Earlier versions (Swipe Puzzle, up to 0.8.0)

The versions published under the name Swipe Puzzle have no privacy notice and none of the analytics or crash reporting described above. They are supported by ads from Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): a banner at the top of the screen and occasional full-screen ads when a level starts. To show, limit, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, Unity Ads may process the advertising identifier and other device identifiers, the IP address and an approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. Unity's privacy policy applies to this processing and describes your choices.

Those versions store the current level, the number of levels started, whether the game has been launched before and the record of a "No ads" purchase on your device. Unity's engine services enabled in them can send device information (such as model, operating system and language), the app version, installation identifiers, basic usage events such as app starts, and crash reports to Unity. Updating the game replaces this behavior with the one described in the sections above; your level and your purchase are kept.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not ask for a date of birth. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the address above.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its services change, in particular before advertising is turned on. The date above identifies the current version. New purposes of data use require a new in-game privacy decision. Questions: easycubeindiegames@gmail.com.
